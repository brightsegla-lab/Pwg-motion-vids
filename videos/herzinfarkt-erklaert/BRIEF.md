## Intent

The first video for the new social page **med2be**, which makes useful videos on medical topics
for medical students in Germany. Topic: Krankheitslehre – Herzinfarkt (Myokardinfarkt),
explained in detail and step by step: risk factors → atherosclerosis → plaque → rupture and
thrombus → ischaemia and necrosis → coronary territories → symptoms → ECG (STEMI/NSTEMI) →
troponin → therapy. The user asked for "max" quality: an advanced motion graphic, not a slideshow.
Narrated in German, with captions.

## Notes

- Medical content follows current ESC guidelines (ACS 2023): O₂ only if SpO₂ < 90 %, STEMI
  → primary PCI ≤ 120 min after diagnosis, hs-troponin 0h/1h algorithm. MONA is no longer
  given routinely.
- The end card carries a small line: "Nur zu Lernzwecken · kein Ersatz für ärztlichen Rat · ESC-Leitlinie ACS 2023".
- 9:16 means one idea per screen, large type and ECG traces that are readable on a phone.
